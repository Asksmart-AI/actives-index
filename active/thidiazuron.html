    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Thidiazuron have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 15 current active constituent approvals cover Thidiazuron. Holders include Bayer Crop Science Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited, Echem (Aust) Pty Limited, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd and Max(Rudong) Chemicals Co., Ltd., plus 6 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Thidiazuron products in Australia?

    When does data protection end for Thidiazuron?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Thidiazuron ends on 2030-10-21. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Thidiazuron registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Thidiazuron most often cover Cotton, Cotton - pre harvest, Cotton - pre-harvest and Cotton defoliation. The label is what applies.
