    How many approved sources does Methyl Bromide have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 6 current active constituent approvals cover Methyl Bromide. Holders include Trical Australia Pty Ltd, Mebrom Pty Ltd, Nufarm Australia Limited and R.A. Dibbs & Sons Pty. Ltd.. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.

    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 5 companies register 9 agricultural products containing Methyl Bromide. The most products are registered by Trical Australia Pty Ltd, Mebrom Pty Ltd and Intech Organics Australia Pty Ltd.
