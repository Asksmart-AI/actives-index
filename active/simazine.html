    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Simazine have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 15 current active constituent approvals cover Simazine. Holders include Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited, Adama Australia Pty Limited, Sipcam Pacific Australia Pty. Limited, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd and Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, plus 8 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Simazine products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 56 companies register 77 agricultural products containing Simazine. The most products are registered by Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited, Titan Ag Pty Ltd and Australian Agribusiness (Holdings) Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Simazine?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, the latest protected data for a product containing Simazine ends on 2028-03-03. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Simazine registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Simazine most often cover Faba bean, Chickpea, Gladiolus, Hops and Lupin, plus 136 more. The label is what applies.
