    Common questions about Fluazinam
    How many approved sources does Fluazinam have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 13 current active constituent approvals cover Fluazinam. Holders include Ishihara Sangyo Kaisha, Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited, FMC Australasia Pty Ltd, Nufarm Australia Limited and PCT Holdings Pty Ltd, plus 5 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Fluazinam products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 12 companies register 16 agricultural products containing Fluazinam. The most products are registered by Ishihara Sangyo Kaisha, Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited and FMC Australasia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Fluazinam?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Fluazinam ends on 2030-01-10. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Fluazinam registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Fluazinam most often cover Cabbage, Brussels sprouts, Broccoli, Cauliflower and Kohlrabi, plus 6 more. The label is what applies.
