    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Trifloxystrobin have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 17 current active constituent approvals cover Trifloxystrobin. Holders include Bayer Crop Science Pty Ltd, United Cropscience Pte., Ltd., Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd, Thai Harvest Limited and Yongnong Biosciences Co., Ltd, plus 11 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Trifloxystrobin products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 6 companies register 11 agricultural products containing Trifloxystrobin. The most products are registered by Bayer Crop Science Pty Ltd, 2022 Environmental Science AU Pty Ltd and Farmalinx Pty Ltd.

    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Trifloxystrobin ends on 2031-03-05. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
