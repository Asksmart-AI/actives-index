    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Pymetrozine have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 9 current active constituent approvals cover Pymetrozine. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.

    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Pymetrozine ends on 2031-04-15. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
