    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Lambda-Cyhalothrin have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 22 current active constituent approvals cover Lambda-Cyhalothrin. Holders include Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Titan Ag Pty Ltd, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, Arch Wood Protection (Aust) Pty Limited and Adama Australia Pty Limited, plus 13 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Lambda-Cyhalothrin products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 32 companies register 44 agricultural products containing Lambda-Cyhalothrin. The most products are registered by Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Sherwood Chemicals Australasia Pty Ltd and Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Lambda-Cyhalothrin?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Lambda-Cyhalothrin ends on 2030-03-17. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Lambda-Cyhalothrin registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Lambda-Cyhalothrin most often cover Canola, Forage brassicae, Field pea, Cotton and Wheat, plus 57 more. The label is what applies.
