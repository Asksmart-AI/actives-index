    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Fludioxonil have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 13 current active constituent approvals cover Fludioxonil. Holders include Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Colin Campbell (Chemicals) Pty Ltd, Farmalinx Pty Ltd, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd and UPL Australia Pty Ltd, plus 6 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Fludioxonil products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 14 companies register 34 agricultural products containing Fludioxonil. The most products are registered by Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited and Colin Campbell (Chemicals) Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Fludioxonil?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Fludioxonil ends on 2032-03-23. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.

    Registered labels containing Fludioxonil most often cover Cut flower, Lettuce, Alliums (incl. bulb onions, spring onions, shallots, garlic), Onion and Citrus, plus 69 more. The label is what applies.
