    Common questions about Cyprodinil
    How many approved sources does Cyprodinil have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 5 current active constituent approvals cover Cyprodinil. Holders include Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd, Tgac Australia Pty. Ltd. and Nanjing Bioagriland Crop Care Co Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Cyprodinil products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 10 companies register 13 agricultural products containing Cyprodinil. The most products are registered by Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited and Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited.
    When does data protection end for Cyprodinil?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Cyprodinil ends on 2032-09-23. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Cyprodinil registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Cyprodinil most often cover Onion, Lettuce, Alliums (incl. bulb onions, spring onions, shallots, garlic), Cut flower and Grape, plus 34 more. The label is what applies.
