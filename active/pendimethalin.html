    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Pendimethalin have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 16 current active constituent approvals cover Pendimethalin. Holders include BASF Australia Ltd., UPL Australia Pty Ltd, Rallis India Limited, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd and Jiangsu Yongan Chemical Co., Ltd, plus 8 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Pendimethalin products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 27 companies register 43 agricultural products containing Pendimethalin. The most products are registered by Australian Agribusiness (Holdings) Pty Ltd, BASF Australia Ltd. and UPL Australia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Pendimethalin?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Pendimethalin ends on 2031-04-15. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Pendimethalin registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Pendimethalin most often cover Deciduous fruit, Nuts, Mango, Avocado and Cotton - irrigation bank, plus 238 more. The label is what applies.
