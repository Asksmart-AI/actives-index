    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Etoxazole have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 3 current active constituent approvals cover Etoxazole. Holders include Sumitomo Chemical Australia Pty. Limited, Thai Harvest Limited and Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Etoxazole products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 3 companies register 5 agricultural products containing Etoxazole. The most products are registered by Sumitomo Chemical Australia Pty. Limited, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd and PCT Holdings Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Etoxazole?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Etoxazole ends on 2032-10-05. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Etoxazole registered for in Australia?
