    When does data protection end for Chlorantraniliprole?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Chlorantraniliprole ends on 2033-12-12. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Chlorantraniliprole registered for in Australia?
