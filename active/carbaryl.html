    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Carbaryl have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 6 current active constituent approvals cover Carbaryl. Holders include David Gray & Co. Pty Limited, Kendon Plant Care Pty. Ltd., Tessenderlo Kerley, Inc, Sinon Australia Pty Limited and Adama New Zealand Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Carbaryl products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 6 companies register 9 agricultural products containing Carbaryl. The most products are registered by David Gray & Co. Pty Limited, Kendon Plant Care Pty. Ltd. and Heiniger Australia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Carbaryl?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Carbaryl has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Carbaryl registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Carbaryl most often cover Ornamental, Mango, Orange, Potato and Duboisia or corkwood, plus 81 more. The label is what applies.
