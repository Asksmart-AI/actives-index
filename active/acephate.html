    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Acephate have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 9 current active constituent approvals cover Acephate. Holders include UPL Australia Pty Ltd, Arysta Lifescience North America Llc, Coromandel Australia Pty Ltd, Sinon Australia Pty Limited and Australian Agribusiness (Holdings) Pty Ltd, plus 2 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Acephate products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 8 companies register 9 agricultural products containing Acephate. The most products are registered by UPL Australia Pty Ltd, Arysta Lifescience North America Llc and Sinon Australia Pty Limited.
    When does data protection end for Acephate?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Acephate has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Acephate registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Acephate most often cover Tobacco, Broccoli, Cauliflower, Brussels sprouts and Ornamental, plus 8 more. The label is what applies.
