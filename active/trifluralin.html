    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Trifluralin have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 32 current active constituent approvals cover Trifluralin. Holders include Gowan Crop Protection Limited, Nufarm Australia Limited, Conquest Crop Protection Pty Ltd, 4 Farmers Australia Pty Ltd and Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd, plus 16 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Trifluralin products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 54 companies register 86 agricultural products containing Trifluralin. The most products are registered by Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited, Titan Ag Pty Ltd and Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Trifluralin?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, the latest protected data for a product containing Trifluralin ends on 2030-06-19. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Trifluralin registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Trifluralin most often cover Wheat, Barley, Canola, Lupin and Pea, plus 184 more. The label is what applies.
