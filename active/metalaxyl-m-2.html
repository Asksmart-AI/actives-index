    Common questions about Metalaxyl-M
    How many approved sources does Metalaxyl-M have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 7 current active constituent approvals cover Metalaxyl-M. Holders include Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Farmalinx Pty Ltd, Nufarm Australia Limited, Turf Culture Pty Ltd and Agchem Australia Pty Ltd, plus 2 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Metalaxyl-M products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 12 companies register 27 agricultural products containing Metalaxyl-M. The most products are registered by Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Farmalinx Pty Ltd and Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited.
    When does data protection end for Metalaxyl-M?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Metalaxyl-M ends on 2030-01-17. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Metalaxyl-M registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Metalaxyl-M most often cover Barley, Wheat, Cotton, Sub clover seed and Maize seed, plus 56 more. The label is what applies.
