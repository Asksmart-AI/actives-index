    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does MCPA have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no approval is recorded under this form. Approvals sit under the parent, MCPA, which has 23 approved sources.
    Who registers MCPA products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 40 companies register 76 agricultural products containing MCPA. The most products are registered by Brunnings Garden Products Pty Ltd, Duluxgroup (Australia) Pty Ltd and Adama Australia Pty Limited.
    When does data protection end for MCPA?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing MCPA ends on 2030-11-03. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is MCPA registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing MCPA most often cover Wheat, Barley, Oats, Triticale and Turf, plus 35 more. The label is what applies.
