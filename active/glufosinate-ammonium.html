Holders include UPL Australia Pty Ltd, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited, Titan Ag Pty Ltd, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd and BASF Australia Ltd., plus 20 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Glufosinate-Ammonium products in Australia?
