    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Glufosinate-Ammonium have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 37 current active constituent approvals cover Glufosinate-Ammonium. Holders include UPL Australia Pty Ltd, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited, Titan Ag Pty Ltd, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd and BASF Australia Ltd., plus 20 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Glufosinate-Ammonium products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 62 companies register 84 agricultural products containing Glufosinate-Ammonium. The most products are registered by UPL Australia Pty Ltd, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited and Titan Ag Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Glufosinate-Ammonium?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Glufosinate-Ammonium ends on 2030-09-18. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Glufosinate-Ammonium registered for in Australia?
