    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Pyrimethanil have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 7 current active constituent approvals cover Pyrimethanil. Holders include Arysta Lifescience Benelux Sprl, Bayer Crop Science Pty Ltd, Grochem Australia Pty Ltd, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd and Adama Australia Pty Limited. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Pyrimethanil products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 8 companies register 11 agricultural products containing Pyrimethanil. The most products are registered by Janssen Pharmaceutica N.V., Agrofresh Australia Pty Ltd and Colin Campbell (Chemicals) Pty Ltd.

    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Pyrimethanil ends on 2030-08-13. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.

    Registered labels containing Pyrimethanil most often cover Strawberry, Grapevine, Banana, Citrus and Apple, plus 7 more. The label is what applies.
