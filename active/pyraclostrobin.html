    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Pyraclostrobin have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 23 current active constituent approvals cover Pyraclostrobin. Holders include BASF Australia Ltd., Grow Choice Pty Ltd, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd and Kenso Corporation (M) Sdn. Bhd., plus 16 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Pyraclostrobin products in Australia?

    When does data protection end for Pyraclostrobin?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Pyraclostrobin ends on 2032-10-02. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
