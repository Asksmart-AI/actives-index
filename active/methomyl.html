    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Methomyl have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 11 current active constituent approvals cover Methomyl. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Methomyl products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 21 companies register 30 agricultural products containing Methomyl. The most products are registered by Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited and Adama Australia Pty Limited.
    When does data protection end for Methomyl?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Methomyl ends on 2029-08-30. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.

    Registered labels containing Methomyl most often cover Navy bean, Long bean, Sorghum, Peanut and Citrus, plus 87 more. The label is what applies.
