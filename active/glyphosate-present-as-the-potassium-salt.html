    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Glyphosate have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, no approval is recorded under this form. Approvals sit under the parent, Glyphosate, which has 83 approved sources.
    Who registers Glyphosate products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 54 companies register 98 agricultural products containing Glyphosate. The most products are registered by Australian Agribusiness (Holdings) Pty Ltd, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited and Sinochem International Australia Pty. Ltd..
    When does data protection end for Glyphosate?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, the latest protected data for a product containing Glyphosate ends on 2035-04-17. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Glyphosate registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Glyphosate most often cover Pasture topping, Adzuki beans - pre-harvest, Field pea - pre-harvest, Mungbean - pre-harvest and Faba bean - pre-harvest, plus 208 more. The label is what applies.
