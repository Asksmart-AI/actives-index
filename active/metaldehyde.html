    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Metaldehyde have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 9 current active constituent approvals cover Metaldehyde. Holders include Triox Pty. Ltd., Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, Arch Wood Protection (Aust) Pty Limited, Pennzoil-Quaker State Australia Company and Adama Australia Pty Limited, plus 4 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Metaldehyde products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 22 companies register 33 agricultural products containing Metaldehyde. The most products are registered by Triox Pty. Ltd., Evergreen Garden Care Australia Pty Ltd and Duluxgroup (Australia) Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Metaldehyde?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, the latest protected data for a product containing Metaldehyde ends on 2030-06-18. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Metaldehyde registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Metaldehyde most often cover Pulse crop, Areas where snails and slugs congregate, Pasture establishment, Vegetable - seedlings & mature crops and Flowers - seedlings & mature crops, plus 52 more. The label is what applies.
