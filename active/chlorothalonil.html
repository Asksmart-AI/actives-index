    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Chlorothalonil have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 23 current active constituent approvals cover Chlorothalonil. Holders include Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Sipcam Pacific Australia Pty. Limited, Adama Australia Pty Limited, Sinon Australia Pty Limited and Titan Ag Pty Ltd, plus 12 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Chlorothalonil products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 49 companies register 86 agricultural products containing Chlorothalonil. The most products are registered by Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited, Colin Campbell (Chemicals) Pty Ltd and Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Chlorothalonil?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, the latest protected data for a product containing Chlorothalonil ends on 2028-09-22. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Chlorothalonil registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Chlorothalonil most often cover Plum, Nectarine, Apricot, Cabbage and Peach, plus 88 more. The label is what applies.
