    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Fenamiphos have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 5 current active constituent approvals cover Fenamiphos. Holders include Agnova Technologies Pty Ltd, 4 Farmers Australia Pty Ltd and Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Fenamiphos products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 9 companies register 10 agricultural products containing Fenamiphos. The most products are registered by Agnova Technologies Pty Ltd, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd and Australian Agribusiness (Holdings) Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Fenamiphos?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Fenamiphos has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Fenamiphos registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Fenamiphos most often cover Banana - planting material, Aloe vera - planting material and Strawberry. The label is what applies.
