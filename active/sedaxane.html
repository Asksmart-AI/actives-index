    How many approved sources does Sedaxane have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 2 current active constituent approvals cover Sedaxane. Holders include Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd and Kingtai Chemicals Co., Limited. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Sedaxane products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 1 company registers 4 agricultural products containing Sedaxane. The most products are registered by Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd.

    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, the latest protected data for a product containing Sedaxane ends on 2028-04-12. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
