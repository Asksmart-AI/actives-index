    Common questions about Carbendazim
    How many approved sources does Carbendazim have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 10 current active constituent approvals cover Carbendazim. Holders include Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, Titan Ag Pty Ltd, 4 Farmers Australia Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited and Sinon Australia Pty Limited, plus 4 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Carbendazim products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 21 companies register 27 agricultural products containing Carbendazim. The most products are registered by Australian Agribusiness (Holdings) Pty Ltd, Titan Ag Pty Ltd and Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Carbendazim?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Carbendazim has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Carbendazim registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Carbendazim most often cover Faba bean, Lentil, Vetch, Chickpea and Macadamia nut, plus 7 more. The label is what applies.
