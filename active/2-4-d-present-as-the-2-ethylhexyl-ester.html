    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does 2,4-D have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 3 current active constituent approvals cover 2,4-D. Holders include Thai Harvest Limited, Profeng Australia Pty Ltd and Pt Trustchem Agro Indonesia. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers 2,4-D products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 25 companies register 41 agricultural products containing 2,4-D. The most products are registered by Adama Australia Pty Limited, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited and Nufarm Australia Limited.
    When does data protection end for 2,4-D?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing 2,4-D ends on 2027-03-18. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is 2,4-D registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing 2,4-D most often cover Triticale, Improved pasture containing clover, Wheat, Pasture - direct drilling/surface sowing and Pasture (non legumes), plus 56 more. The label is what applies.
