    Common questions about Dimethoate
    How many approved sources does Dimethoate have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 8 current active constituent approvals cover Dimethoate. Holders include FMC Australasia Pty Ltd, Farmalinx Pty Ltd, Sinon Australia Pty Limited, Agrogill Chemicals Pty Ltd and Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Dimethoate products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 13 companies register 14 agricultural products containing Dimethoate. The most products are registered by Titan Ag Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited and 4 Farmers Australia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Dimethoate?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Dimethoate has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Dimethoate registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Dimethoate most often cover Adzuki bean, Pasture seed and forage crops, Sorghum, Ornamentals (see label for exceptions) and Protea, plus 89 more. The label is what applies.
