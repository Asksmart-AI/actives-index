    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Clethodim have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 31 current active constituent approvals cover Clethodim. Holders include Sumitomo Chemical Australia Pty. Limited, Arysta Lifescience North America Llc, Adama Australia Pty Limited, Titan Ag Pty Ltd and Kenso Corporation (M) Sdn. Bhd., plus 20 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Clethodim products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 59 companies register 103 agricultural products containing Clethodim. The most products are registered by Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited, Sumitomo Chemical Australia Pty. Limited and Arysta Lifescience North America Llc.
    When does data protection end for Clethodim?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Clethodim ends on 2029-10-11. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Clethodim registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Clethodim most often cover Lettuce, Cabbage, Non bearing fruit tree, Beetroot and Celery, plus 50 more. The label is what applies.
