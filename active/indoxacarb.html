    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Indoxacarb have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 17 current active constituent approvals cover Indoxacarb. Holders include FMC Australasia Pty Ltd, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd, Gharda Australia Pty. Ltd., Sundew Solutions Pty Ltd and Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, plus 7 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Indoxacarb products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 21 companies register 33 agricultural products containing Indoxacarb. The most products are registered by Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, FMC Australasia Pty Ltd and AU Pest Pty Limited.
    When does data protection end for Indoxacarb?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Indoxacarb ends on 2027-05-31. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Indoxacarb registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Indoxacarb most often cover Soybean, Adzuki bean, Faba bean, Mung bean and Cabbage - closed headed varieties only, plus 100 more. The label is what applies.
