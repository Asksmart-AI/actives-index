    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Captan have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 15 current active constituent approvals cover Captan. Holders include Adama Australia Pty Limited, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, Arysta Lifescience North America Llc, Grochem Australia Pty Ltd and Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd, plus 7 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Captan products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 19 companies register 26 agricultural products containing Captan. The most products are registered by Cropsure Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited and Kenso Corporation (M) Sdn. Bhd..
    When does data protection end for Captan?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Captan has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Captan registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Captan most often cover Strawberry, Tobacco seedling, Turf, Pear and Rose, plus 9 more. The label is what applies.
