    How many approved sources does Malathion have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 4 current active constituent approvals cover Malathion. Holders include FMC Australasia Pty Ltd, Gulmohar Chemicals Pty Ltd, Tagros Chemicals India Private Limited and Ningbo Sunjoy Agroscience Co., Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Malathion products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 5 companies register 11 agricultural products containing Malathion. The most products are registered by David Gray & Co. Pty Limited, FMC Australasia Pty Ltd and Bugs For Bugs Pty Ltd.

    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, the latest protected data for a product containing Malathion ends on 2032-05-02. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
