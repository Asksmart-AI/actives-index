    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Linuron have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 8 current active constituent approvals cover Linuron. Holders include Tessenderlo Kerley, Inc, Adama Australia Pty Limited, Finchimica S.P.A. and Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Linuron products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 6 companies register 8 agricultural products containing Linuron. The most products are registered by Tessenderlo Kerley, Inc, Sharda Australia Pty Ltd and Adama Australia Pty Limited.
    When does data protection end for Linuron?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Linuron has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Linuron registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Linuron most often cover Barley, Oats, Potato, Maize and Sweet corn, plus 18 more. The label is what applies.
