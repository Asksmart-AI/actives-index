    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Glyphosate have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 83 current active constituent approvals cover Glyphosate. Holders include Nufarm Australia Limited, Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited, Sinochem International Australia Pty. Ltd. and Lief Petroleum Pty Ltd, plus 41 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Glyphosate products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 13 companies register 19 agricultural products containing Glyphosate. The most products are registered by Duluxgroup (Australia) Pty Ltd, Alpha Crop Protection Pty Ltd and Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Glyphosate?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, the latest protected data for a product containing Glyphosate ends on 2031-04-17. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Glyphosate registered for in Australia?
