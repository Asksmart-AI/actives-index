    Common questions about Boscalid
    How many approved sources does Boscalid have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 15 current active constituent approvals cover Boscalid. Holders include BASF Australia Ltd., Farmalinx Pty Ltd, Grow Choice Pty Ltd, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd and Colin Campbell (Chemicals) Pty Ltd, plus 8 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Boscalid products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 21 companies register 27 agricultural products containing Boscalid. The most products are registered by BASF Australia Ltd., Farmalinx Pty Ltd and Sharda Australia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Boscalid?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Boscalid ends on 2031-05-13. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Boscalid registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Boscalid most often cover Grapevine, Apple, Pear, Turf and Onion, plus 17 more. The label is what applies.
