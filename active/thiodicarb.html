    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Thiodicarb have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 6 current active constituent approvals cover Thiodicarb. Holders include Bayer Crop Science Pty Ltd, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd and Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Thiodicarb products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 4 companies register 4 agricultural products containing Thiodicarb. The most products are registered by Adama Australia Pty Limited, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd and Bayer Crop Science Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Thiodicarb?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Thiodicarb has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Thiodicarb registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Thiodicarb most often cover Cotton, Tobacco, Sweet corn, Maize and Brassica spp. or cole crop, plus 8 more. The label is what applies.
