    Common questions about Metalaxyl
    How many approved sources does Metalaxyl have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 13 current active constituent approvals cover Metalaxyl. Holders include UPL Australia Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd, Bayer Crop Science Pty Ltd and Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, plus 7 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Metalaxyl products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 14 companies register 29 agricultural products containing Metalaxyl. The most products are registered by UPL Australia Pty Ltd, Titan Ag Pty Ltd and Farmalinx Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Metalaxyl?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Metalaxyl ends on 2027-08-09. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Metalaxyl registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Metalaxyl most often cover Grapevine, Cucurbit, Potato, Macadamia nut and Tobacco seedbed, plus 35 more. The label is what applies.
