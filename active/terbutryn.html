    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Terbutryn have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 7 current active constituent approvals cover Terbutryn. Holders include Titan Ag Pty Ltd, Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited, Agrogill Chemicals Pty Ltd and Rainbow Agroscience Australia Pty Ltd, plus 2 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Terbutryn products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 16 companies register 24 agricultural products containing Terbutryn. The most products are registered by Titan Ag Pty Ltd, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited and Nufarm Australia Limited.
    When does data protection end for Terbutryn?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Terbutryn has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Terbutryn registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Terbutryn most often cover Barley, Wheat, Oats - see label for varieties, Sugar cane and Subclover pasture - see lbl 4 varieties, plus 23 more. The label is what applies.
