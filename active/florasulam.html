    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Florasulam have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 12 current active constituent approvals cover Florasulam. Holders include Corteva Agriscience Australia Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited, FMC Australasia Pty Ltd, Conquest Crop Protection Pty Ltd and Yongnong Biosciences Co., Ltd, plus 5 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Florasulam products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 7 companies register 11 agricultural products containing Florasulam. The most products are registered by Corteva Agriscience Australia Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited and FMC Australasia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Florasulam?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Florasulam ends on 2030-08-27. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Florasulam registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Florasulam most often cover Barley, Triticale, Wheat, Fallow - see label and Oats - see label, plus 14 more. The label is what applies.
