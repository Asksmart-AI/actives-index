    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Thiram have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 7 current active constituent approvals cover Thiram. Holders include Arysta Lifescience Australia Pty Ltd, Grochem Australia Pty Ltd, Eastman Chemical Australia Pty Ltd, Nufarm Australia Limited and Gulmohar Chemicals Pty Ltd, plus 2 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Thiram products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 16 companies register 40 agricultural products containing Thiram. The most products are registered by Resene Paints (Australia) Limited, Arysta Lifescience Australia Pty Ltd and Australian Agribusiness (Holdings) Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Thiram?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Thiram has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Thiram registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Thiram most often cover Turf, Commercial vessel(steel,timber,fibreglas, Sorghum seed - for export only, Boat - exterior (antifouling) and Ferro-cement hull, plus 64 more. The label is what applies.
