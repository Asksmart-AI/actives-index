    How many approved sources does Ethephon have in Australia?

A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.

    When does data protection end for Ethephon?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Ethephon has data protection running past 2026-10-31.

    Registered labels containing Ethephon most often cover Pineapple, Tomato, Tydeman's early apple, Granny smith apple and Lady williams apple, plus 46 more. The label is what applies.
