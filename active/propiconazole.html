    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Propiconazole have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 32 current active constituent approvals cover Propiconazole. Holders include Arch Wood Protection (Aust) Pty Limited, Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited and Titan Ag Pty Ltd, plus 21 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Propiconazole products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 51 companies register 101 agricultural products containing Propiconazole. The most products are registered by Arch Wood Protection (Aust) Pty Limited, Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd and Koppers Performance Chemicals Australia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Propiconazole?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Propiconazole ends on 2030-10-28. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Propiconazole registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Propiconazole most often cover Wheat, Barley, Oats, Peanut and Stone fruit, plus 57 more. The label is what applies.
