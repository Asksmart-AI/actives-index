    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Atrazine have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 21 current active constituent approvals cover Atrazine. Holders include Adama Australia Pty Limited, Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Sipcam Pacific Australia Pty. Limited, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited and Sinon Australia Pty Limited, plus 13 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Atrazine products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 47 companies register 77 agricultural products containing Atrazine. The most products are registered by Adama Australia Pty Limited, Titan Ag Pty Ltd and Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Atrazine?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Atrazine ends on 2030-07-22. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Atrazine registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Atrazine most often cover Roadside weed control, Eucalyptus plantation, Sugar cane, Pinus plantation - pinus radiata and Canola-triazine tolerant only-post emerg, plus 88 more. The label is what applies.
