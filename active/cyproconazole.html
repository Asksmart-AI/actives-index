    How many approved sources does Cyproconazole have in Australia?

Holders include Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Shandong Rainbow International Co Ltd, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, Sharda Worldwide Exports Pvt Ltd and Jiangsu Flag Chemical Industry Co Ltd, plus 7 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Cyproconazole products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 13 companies register 16 agricultural products containing Cyproconazole. The most products are registered by Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd, Titan Ag Pty Ltd and Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited.
    When does data protection end for Cyproconazole?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Cyproconazole ends on 2028-09-11. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
