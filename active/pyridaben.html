    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Pyridaben have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 5 current active constituent approvals cover Pyridaben. Holders include Nissan Chemical Corporation, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd and Shandong United Pesticide Industry Co., Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Pyridaben products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 1 company registers 1 agricultural product containing Pyridaben. The most products are registered by Nissan Chemical Corporation.
    When does data protection end for Pyridaben?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, no product containing Pyridaben has data protection running past 2026-10-23.
    Which crops is Pyridaben registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Pyridaben most often cover Nashi (asian pear) - nijisseiki, Pear, Grape, Apple crop = apple orchard and Banana, plus 1 more. The label is what applies.
