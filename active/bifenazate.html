    How many approved sources does Bifenazate have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 6 current active constituent approvals cover Bifenazate. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.

    When does data protection end for Bifenazate?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Bifenazate ends on 2030-10-01. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
