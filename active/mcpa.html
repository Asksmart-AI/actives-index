    Common questions about MCPA
    How many approved sources does MCPA have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 23 current active constituent approvals cover MCPA. Holders include Nufarm Australia Limited, BASF Australia Ltd., Adama Australia Pty Limited, Runge Agrichems Pty Ltd and UPL Australia Pty Ltd, plus 14 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers MCPA products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 3 companies register 5 agricultural products containing MCPA. The most products are registered by Duluxgroup (Australia) Pty Ltd, Brunnings Garden Products Pty Ltd and New Australia Agricultural Development Company Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for MCPA?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing MCPA ends on 2027-12-14. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is MCPA registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing MCPA most often cover Shed (in or around), Driveway, Spot spraying in lawns & turf, Path and Garden, plus 8 more. The label is what applies.
