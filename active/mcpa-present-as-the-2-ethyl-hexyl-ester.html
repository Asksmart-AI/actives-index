    Common questions about MCPA
    How many approved sources does MCPA have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 2 current active constituent approvals cover MCPA. Holders include Titan Ag Pty Ltd and Shanghai Hanfu Biotechnology Co., Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers MCPA products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 39 companies register 56 agricultural products containing MCPA. The most products are registered by Australian Agribusiness (Holdings) Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited and Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited.
    When does data protection end for MCPA?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing MCPA ends on 2030-03-03. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is MCPA registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing MCPA most often cover Cereal rye, Triticale, Barley, Wheat and Oats, plus 45 more. The label is what applies.
