    How many approved sources does Cyanazine have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 5 current active constituent approvals cover Cyanazine. Holders include BASF Australia Ltd., Gulmohar Chemicals Pty Ltd and Shandong Binnong Technology Co Ltd. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.

    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 7 companies register 7 agricultural products containing Cyanazine. The most products are registered by Agnova Technologies Pty Ltd, Arch Wood Protection (Aust) Pty Limited and Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited.

    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Cyanazine has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
