    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Triasulfuron have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 8 current active constituent approvals cover Triasulfuron. Holders include Sipcam Pacific Australia Pty. Limited, Conquest Crop Protection Pty Ltd, Agricultural Product Services Pty Ltd, Syngenta Australia Pty Ltd and Nufarm Australia Limited, plus 2 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Triasulfuron products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 27 companies register 31 agricultural products containing Triasulfuron. The most products are registered by Titan Ag Pty Ltd, Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited and Jiangsu Agrochem Laboratory Co Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Triasulfuron?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, no product containing Triasulfuron has data protection running past 2026-10-31.
    Which crops is Triasulfuron registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Triasulfuron most often cover Barley - post emergence, Wheat - post emergent, Oats - post-emergence, Wheat - pre-emergent and Triticale - post-emergence, plus 13 more. The label is what applies.
