    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Deltamethrin have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 18 current active constituent approvals cover Deltamethrin. Holders include Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, Adama Australia Pty Limited, Sinon Australia Pty Limited, Bayer Crop Science Pty Ltd and Gharda Australia Pty. Ltd., plus 10 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Deltamethrin products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 27 companies register 65 agricultural products containing Deltamethrin. The most products are registered by 2022 Environmental Science AU Pty Ltd, Australian Agribusiness (Holdings) Pty Ltd and S.C. Johnson & Son Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Deltamethrin?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Deltamethrin ends on 2030-08-29. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Deltamethrin registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Deltamethrin most often cover Pine logs - prior to debarking, Commercial premises - general, House - general, Hospital and Freshly felled logs, poles, posts, plus 111 more. The label is what applies.
