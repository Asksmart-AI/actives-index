    How many approved sources does Flonicamid have in Australia?

Holders include Ishihara Sangyo Kaisha, Ltd, Cropnosys India Private Limited, Shandong Jingbo Agrochemicals Technology Co., Ltd., Agrobeats Tech Co. Ltd. and Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, plus 3 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Flonicamid products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, 6 companies register 9 agricultural products containing Flonicamid. The most products are registered by Ishihara Sangyo Kaisha, Ltd, Grochem Australia Pty Ltd and Colin Campbell (Chemicals) Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Flonicamid?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-31, the latest protected data for a product containing Flonicamid ends on 2031-04-17. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Flonicamid registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Flonicamid most often cover Cotton, Squash, Cucurbit, Strawberries - open field and protected cropping and Cucumber, plus 13 more. The label is what applies.
