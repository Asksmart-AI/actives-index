    Quick answers

    How many approved sources does Alpha-Cypermethrin have in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 20 current active constituent approvals cover Alpha-Cypermethrin. Holders include Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd, BASF Australia Ltd., Gharda Australia Pty. Ltd., Colin Campbell (Chemicals) Pty Ltd and Halley International Enterprise (Australia) Pty Ltd, plus 14 more. A holder is not necessarily the manufacturer.
    Who registers Alpha-Cypermethrin products in Australia?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, 42 companies register 60 agricultural products containing Alpha-Cypermethrin. The most products are registered by Nutrien Ag Solutions Limited, Imtrade Australia Pty Ltd and Titan Ag Pty Ltd.
    When does data protection end for Alpha-Cypermethrin?
    As of the APVMA export downloaded 2026-10-23, the latest protected data for a product containing Alpha-Cypermethrin ends on 2031-09-28. Protection covers specific data, not the active itself.
    Which crops is Alpha-Cypermethrin registered for in Australia?
    Registered labels containing Alpha-Cypermethrin most often cover Sweet corn, Sorghum, Field pea, Cotton and Maize, plus 102 more. The label is what applies.
